    How each number is worked out
    Growth (actual)
    From your holdings screenshots: each period’s return = value at end ÷ (value at start + new money added) − 1, chained from 100. Deposits are left out, so it shows how the money invested did, not how much the balance grew. Only the % is stored, never {{money_word}}.

    vs {{cash}} / vs inflation
    Your growth minus what the same money would have done left in {{cash}} (its % a year × days ÷ 365), or minus inflation (year-on-year CPI scaled to the days), over the same dates as your holdings snapshots. No verdict until 12 months of data; after that, Winning or Losing only beyond ±1 point.
    {{def_equity}}
    Moved toward target
    1 − (still to move ÷ still to move at start). “Still to move” is half the sum of |now − target| across {{funds}}. A planned step back (like consolidating first) is marked Planned, not Lose.
